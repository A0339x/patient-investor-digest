# LP Mastermind Weekly Digest — Style Guide & Format Reference

## Audience
Concentrated liquidity providers running Uniswap V3 and V4 positions. They set price ranges to earn trading fees. They are NOT directional traders — macro and price action matter only as context for range-setting (volatility expectations, sudden out-of-range risk, fee volume opportunities). Write for practitioners.

---

## What They Care About (Priority Order)
1. **Volatility** — Was the week spiky or calm? Did typical ranges hold or get knocked out? What's the volatility regime heading into next week?
2. **Volume & Fees** — Is there more or less on-chain swap activity? Which pairs/pools are generating strong fees right now?
3. **Protocol Updates** — Uniswap V3/V4 news, new hooks, new pools, gas costs (gas directly affects rebalancing economics)
4. **Macro as Context** — Inflation, dollar, Fed, geopolitics — only useful insofar as it affects volatility or on-chain volume
5. **Liquidity Landscape** — Institutional capital entering pools, TVL shifts, yield compression or expansion in DeFi
6. **Regulatory** — Only if it directly affects on-chain activity or LP confidence

---

## Newsletter Structure

### Header
```
💧 LP Mastermind Market Update — [Date]
*What moved this week, what it means for your ranges, and what's worth talking about.*
```

### Week at a Glance (1–3 lines)
Quick snapshot: key price moves for BTC/ETH, volatility characterization (spike/calm/ranging), ETH gas cost. Frame it through the LP lens — e.g. "Tight ranges got knocked out mid-week, wider positions held" not "BTC is up X%."

### 4–6 Stories
Each story follows this exact format:

**## [Number]. [Punchy, LP-relevant title]**

2–3 sentences in plain English. Lead with what happened, then connect explicitly to what it means for someone managing a V3/V4 range position. Use language like:
- "If you were running tight ranges..."
- "For LPs, this means..."
- "Rebalancing costs, fee volumes, range width decisions..."

**> 💬 For the group:** [One question that gets people sharing their actual setups, current pairs, range decisions, or strategy thinking. Not "what do you think about X" — ask for specifics like widths, pairs, rebalancing frequency.]

### Footer
```
*What are you watching going into next week? Drop your current setups, pairs, and range thoughts in the group.*
```

---

## Tone
- Practitioner-to-practitioner, not analyst-to-reader
- Assume they know what impermanent loss, fee tiers, and concentrated liquidity are — don't over-explain
- Brief over comprehensive — they're busy managers, not researchers
- Conversation-first — every story should make someone want to reply with their setup

## What to Avoid
- Price predictions or directional calls ("ETH looks bullish...")
- Generic crypto market commentary not connected to LP mechanics
- Long regulatory summaries unless they unlock/restrict on-chain activity directly
- More than 6 stories — brevity is the point

---

## Research Topics (Each Run)
1. Crypto price action and volatility — what moved, how sharply, did it settle or stay erratic
2. Uniswap V3/V4 — protocol updates, new pools, hooks, volume, TVL
3. On-chain activity — gas fees, swap volumes, DEX activity
4. Macro volatility context — CPI, Fed, geopolitical events with market-moving impact
5. Institutional/DeFi capital — big players entering LP markets, yield trends, TVL shifts

---

## Example Story (Reference)

### ✅ Good
**## 1. Volatility Spike, Then Whipsaw — How Did Your Ranges Hold?**
The Middle East ceasefire on April 7–8 triggered a fast 5–6% move up in ETH almost overnight, following a week of macro-driven suppression. If you were running tight ranges through the spike, you likely went out of range and missed the fee window. If you were running wider ranges anticipating macro uncertainty, this was a solid fee-generating week.
> **💬 For the group:** What range widths are you running right now given the macro backdrop — tighter for fees, or wider for resilience? Did anyone get caught out of range during the April 7 move?

### ❌ Too generic
**Ethereum Rises 5.6% on Ceasefire News**
Ethereum gained this week as a ceasefire in the Middle East boosted risk sentiment. Analysts are bullish on ETH heading into Q2.
